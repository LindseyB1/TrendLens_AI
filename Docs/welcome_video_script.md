# TrendLens AI Welcome Video Script

Welcome to TrendLens AI. This app helps you turn public updates into one clear report.

Step 1: Pick who the report is for. You can choose a student, analyst, responder, or public audience.

Step 2: Tell the app why you need the report. One short sentence is enough.

Step 3: Paste public sources. You can use articles, alerts, reports, or updates. Do not paste private, secret, or sensitive information.

Step 4: Choose the report parts you want. You can select a BLUF, summary, confidence, risks, RFIs, and a 45-second brief.

Step 5: Click Generate TrendLens Report. The model asks to use the tool called analyze_public_sources before it writes the final report.

Step 6: Read the report. Check the source comparison, confidence, gaps, and follow-up questions.

Step 7: Save your work. You can save the report, download it, and save an evaluation record.

Remember: TrendLens AI helps organize public information. It does not prove facts by itself. A person should still review the report.
