# Sample Public Sources: Chemical Spill Test

## Source 1: Local Public Safety Update

City officials reported a chemical spill near an industrial facility on the east side of the city. Roads near the facility were closed while crews assessed the scene. Officials asked the public to avoid the area.

## Source 2: Company Statement

The company stated that the spill was contained on site and that cleanup crews were working with local emergency officials. The company said there was no confirmed off-site impact at the time of the statement.

## Source 3: County Emergency Management Update

County emergency management issued public guidance asking residents to avoid the area until further notice. Fire officials confirmed two nearby businesses were evacuated as a precaution while crews evaluated air quality and traffic control needs.
